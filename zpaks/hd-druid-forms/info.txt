------------------------------------
"Cohesive Druid Forms" by Leeviathan
-----------for WoW 3.3.5a-----------
This patch replaces the original WoW Druid Forms with more visually cohesive ones. This is not strictly a downport from later expansions, I've modified many of the models and textures.

-------------
Installation;
-------------
Copy patch-L.MPQ to your "Data" Folder. 
	Note: Uses modified localized DBC files to make Travel Form make a stag noise rather than a cat noise. If you have issues with spell names disappearing in your non-English client use NON-ENGLISH_patch-L.MPQ instead (rename to "patch-L.MPQ").

OPTIONAL, PICK ONE: 
	-NIGHTELF_patch-M.MPQ This patch makes Travel Form, Aquatic Form, and Tree of Life Form look more NightElf-y (rename to "patch-M.MPQ")
	-TAUREN_patch-M.MPQ This patch makes Travel Form, Aquatic Form, and Tree of Life Form look more Tauren-y (rename to "patch-M.MPQ")

OPTIONAL, PICK ONE: 
	-BrokenNPC_patch-N.MPQ makes this mod compatible with my "WoD Character Models" optional "Broken" NPC-only mod, which updates Broken NPCs to high-poly custom male and female broken models (rename to "patch-N.MPQ")
	-BrokenPlayer_patch-N.MPQ makes this mod compatible with my "WoD Character Models" optional "Broken" Draenei-to-Broken mod, which updates Broken NPCs and Draenei players to high-poly custom male and female broken models (rename to "patch-N.MPQ")
